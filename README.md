- **Problem:** Solo business owners lose significant time on repetitive operational tasks — scheduling, follow-ups, reminders, invoicing, customer communication, and task organization.
- **Target user:** Solo entrepreneurs, freelancers, coaches, consultants, creators, and one-person online businesses.

## Core AI Logic

**We are designing an AI micro-operations assistant for solo business owners because repetitive administrative tasks reduce productivity, increase stress, and limit business growth.**

- **AI does:** generate, summarize, recommend, monitor, route
- **If AI disappeared:** manual fallback still works (spreadsheets, email, calendar)
- **If AI collapsed:** automated reminders, workflow prioritization, follow-ups, and daily organization become slower and inconsistent
- **Hidden dependency:** Calendar, email, and messaging integrations; AI suggestion quality and trust
